# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want HPSS to be a welcoming and constructive open-source research project. Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith, focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people, and give actionable feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory comments, deliberate disruption, or publishing private information without permission.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, please open a private report through GitHub's repository reporting mechanisms when available, or contact the project maintainer through the contact information listed in the repository profile. Please do not include sensitive personal information in public issues.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a respectful project environment.
